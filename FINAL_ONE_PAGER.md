# Ledger Witness

## The trust layer for agentic AI

### The core idea

AI is moving from chat to action. That is the opportunity — and the risk.

Most AI agents still act without a clear line between recommendation and authority. They can read secrets, propose actions, and sometimes execute them without any verifiable human approval.

Ledger Witness changes that.

It gives the agent a strict operating model:

- read and reason
- score and explain risk
- ask for approval
- require a Ledger-backed signature before privileged execution
- log everything for accountability

### Why this is compelling

This is not a wallet demo. This is an AI security architecture for the next generation of autonomous tools.

The product is built around a simple truth:

AI should help us decide faster, not act without accountability.

### What it does

Ledger Witness is a secure execution layer for high-stakes digital actions.

Examples:

- treasury and fund movement approval
- credential rotation and access revocation
- policy-based identity and account recovery workflows
- enterprise AI workflows that need explicit human authorization
- sensitive healthcare data access and consent-gated medical escalation
- any system where trust must be attributable to a real person

### Why Ledger matters

Ledger becomes the proof layer:

- secure secrets stay protected
- approvals take place on-device
- every sensitive action is human-attributable
- users see exactly what is being approved before the action happens

This is the missing primitive for safe agentic systems.

### The product statement

"AI can think and advise. Ledger proves authorization. Together, they create a trusted operating model for the age of autonomous software."

### The winning narrative for the judges

We built an AI agent that can reason, explain, and recommend — but never execute a sensitive action without a user-approved Ledger signature.

That does three things at once:

1. uses Ledger primitives in a real implementation
2. solves a real AI trust problem
3. opens a strong new use case beyond basic crypto workflows, including privacy-sensitive healthcare and identity systems

### Submission hook

"Ledger Witness turns AI from an unchecked executor into a trusted operator — even in the most sensitive environments."
