# Ledger Witness

## The AI that can think, but never act without a human signature

---

## Slide 1 — Title

### Ledger Witness

### A trust layer for AI-powered decisions

Voiceover:

## Slide 2 — The problem

### AI agents are moving faster than trust can follow

- AI is already automating decisions across personal and business workflows
- Models can leak secrets, overreach, and act without accountability
- The risk is not just bad output — it is unauthorized execution

Key message:
"Autonomy without verification is the real AI security problem."

---

## Slide 3 — Why current systems fail

### Most AI apps are just fancy wrappers

- secret exposure
- no explicit human approval
- no verifiable audit trail
- no clear boundary between recommendation and execution

Key message:
"Today's AI is often fast, but not trustworthy."

---

## Slide 4 — Our product

### Ledger Witness adds a trust boundary to AI

- reads and reasons over requests
- scores risk based on policy
- asks for approval for privileged actions
- requires Ledger-backed confirmation for execution
- records signed audit data for accountability

Key message:
"AI can assist the decision, but Ledger proves the authorization."

---

## Slide 5 — Product flow

### Intent → Policy → Approval → Signed execution

1. User asks the AI to act
2. Agent evaluates the action and risk level
3. Policy engine marks it as safe, medium, or critical
4. Critical actions require Ledger Signer approval
5. Only then does the system execute

## Speaker notes (short version)

### 30-second pitch

"We built Ledger Witness, an AI-powered trust layer for high-risk digital actions. The agent can reason, plan, and explain, but it cannot execute privileged actions without a user-approved Ledger signature. That gives us a secure boundary for AI: human intent, Ledger trust, and full accountability. The same model extends naturally to healthcare and privacy-sensitive workflows, where the AI helps without exposing secrets or acting without explicit authorization."

### 60-second pitch

"Most AI systems fail because they are overpowered but under-governed. They can leak secrets, act without review, and provide no clear proof of authorization. Ledger Witness solves that by combining an AI decision layer with a policy engine and a Ledger-backed approval flow. The agent proposes, the policy checks risk, and the Ledger device verifies the human's approval before any privileged action executes. That turns AI from an unchecked automation engine into a trusted operator — and in fields like healthcare, privacy, and enterprise security, that trust boundary is everything."
