# Demo script for the live presentation

## Opening hook

"The real AI problem is not intelligence — it is trust. AI systems are getting better at taking action, but most still act without a verifiable human boundary."

## Product explanation

"Ledger Sentinel is a secure operating layer for AI-powered decisions. It lets the agent read, reason, explain, and propose actions, but refuses to execute privileged actions without an explicit Ledger-backed approval."

## Walkthrough

"Here we see the agent evaluating a risky action. It classifies the task as critical and packages the action into a readable approval request. The user sees: what exactly is being approved, why the system thinks it is necessary, and what risk level it carries."

"Then the approval request is sent to the Ledger device. The user confirms the action on-device, which creates a hardware-backed signature. That signature is then attached to the exact authorized intent and recorded in the application's audit trail."

## Why it matters

"This means the AI never acts outside its policy boundaries, and every high-stakes action remains attributable to a real human. In other words, we are using Ledger as the trust layer for intelligent automation."

## Closing line

"AI can accelerate workflow. Ledger can prove authorization. Together, they create a model where digital actions are both faster and trustworthy."
